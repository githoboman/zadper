# Zadper Officially Launched on BOT Chain Mainnet

**San Francisco, CA — September 2026** — Zadper, the verification layer for autonomous AI agent payments, has officially launched on BOT Chain Mainnet. This deployment marks a major milestone for the agent economy, introducing a secure, low-cost, and tamper-proof audit trail for machine-to-machine transactions.

As AI agents increasingly negotiate, authorize, and execute payments autonomously, the need for transparent verification has become critical. Zadper solves this by providing a unified protocol where AI payment decisions are cryptographically verified against predefined spending policies and securely anchored on-chain.

"The AI agent economy requires infrastructure built specifically for the trustless, high-frequency nature of autonomous transactions," said the team behind Zadper. "By deploying the AgentPayRegistry on BOT Chain Mainnet, we're giving agents the ability to prove exactly why a payment was made, without sacrificing speed or incurring prohibitive gas costs."

## Why BOT Chain?
BOT Chain is an EVM-compatible Layer 1 blockchain explicitly designed for AI Agents, DePIN, and verifiable computing. Its high performance, low transaction fees, and robust ecosystem make it the ideal foundation for Zadper's verification engine.

Through this deployment, any payment processed via the Zadper protocol is recorded on BOT Chain, creating an immutable history that can be audited by any party. This "Don't Trust, Verify" approach ensures that rogue agents or compromised services cannot drain funds unnoticed.

## Available Now
Developers can immediately begin integrating Zadper into their AI applications using the Zadper SDK. The mainnet contract is live and fully functional.

For more information, visit the [Zadper GitHub Repository](https://github.com/githoboman/zadper) or check out our deployment on the [BOT Chain Mainnet Explorer](https://scan.botchain.ai).

**About Zadper**
Zadper (formerly AgentPay) is an open-source protocol for verifiable AI agent payments. It acts as the "arcane eye" of agentic commerce, scoring charges against granular policies and anchoring the verdicts on-chain before a wallet ever signs the transaction.

**About BOT Chain**
BOT Chain is a high-performance, EVM-compatible Layer 1 blockchain purpose-built for AI Agents and the DePIN ecosystem, enabling the protocol economy with secure, low-cost verifiable computing. Learn more at [botchain.ai](https://botchain.ai).
